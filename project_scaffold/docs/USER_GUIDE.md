# AuraSkin AI Skin Intelligence & Personalized Skincare Planner
## Comprehensive User & Clinician Guide

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1. Registration & Roles
1. Open the AuraSkin application at [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).
2. Click **Sign In / Register** in the top navigation bar.
3. Select your desired account role:
   - **User**: For personalized skincare planning, product matching, and longitudinal tracking.
   - **Skincare Consultant / Dermatologist**: For clinical review and client consultation management.
4. Complete your initial account registration.

### 1.2. Completing Your Skin & Lifestyle Profile
1. Navigate to the **Data Entry** tab.
2. Select your **Skin Type** (*Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive*).
3. Specify primary and secondary concerns (*Acne, Hyperpigmentation, Barrier Repair, Aging, Redness*).
4. Record known **Allergies** and **Sensitivities** to ensure automated safety exclusion filtering.
5. Log your daily **Hydration** (ml) and **Sleep Duration** (hours & quality).

---

## 2. Dermal Assessment & AuraScore

1. In the **Dashboard** or **Data Entry** page, click **Run Dermal Assessment**.
2. AuraSkin analyzes your biometric telemetry and calculates your 5-factor **AuraScore (0–100)**:
   - **Skin Condition Score** (out of 35 pts)
   - **Lifestyle Score** (out of 20 pts)
   - **Sleep Score** (out of 15 pts)
   - **Routine Consistency Score** (out of 20 pts)
   - **Hydration Score** (out of 10 pts)
3. Review your tailored **AM / PM / Weekly Chronobiology Regimen**.

---

## 3. Product Discovery & INCI Formula Scanner

### 3.1. Personalized Product Suitability
- Navigate to **Products** to discover biocompatible skincare items matched to your skin profile.
- Filter by Category (*Cleanser, Toner, Serum, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, Treatment*), Budget, and Safety status.
- Click **Add to Routine** to automatically incorporate suitable products into your AM or PM regimen.

### 3.2. 4-Tier INCI Formula Scanner
- Navigate to **Ingredients**.
- Paste any cosmetic ingredient list (INCI string) into the scanner.
- The scanner highlights allergens, checks cross-ingredient conflicts (e.g., *Retinol + AHAs*), and displays evidence ratings.

---

## 4. Notifications & Smart Reminders

- Click the **Bell Icon** in the top navigation bar to open the **Notification Center**.
- **Check Reminders**: Automatically evaluates your today's regimen and reminds you of unlogged AM/PM steps, sub-target hydration, or sleep restoration needs.
- **Notification Preferences**: Customize delivery channels (In-App, Email) and fine-tune reminder categories.

---

## 5. Clinical Reports & PDF/Excel Exports

- Navigate to the **Reports** tab to inspect the full 18-part clinical intelligence analysis.
- Click **Download PDF** to export a formatted, confidential clinical PDF document.
- Click **Export Excel** to download complete longitudinal telemetry, scores, and adherence spreadsheets.

---

## 6. Specialist & Admin Portals

### 6.1. Professional Portal (Consultants & Dermatologists)
- Access verified client directory.
- Review client assessments, AuraScore history, and progress snapshots.
- Respond to direct client consultation requests.

### 6.2. Administrator Portal
- Access platform-level metrics: total users, active verifications, assessment counts, and adherence logs.
- Verify pending specialist and dermatologist credentials.
- Audit real-time system operational health.
